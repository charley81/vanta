Proposed decision

Keep the lab narrow and musically consequential.

If the walkthroughs prove that MORPH retains a recognisable identity while CUTOFF, RESONANCE, DRIVE, ATTACK, and RELEASE give meaningful agency, this is enough. Do not activate OSC, MIX, MOD, LFO, TONE, DECAY, or SUSTAIN just because their knobs are modeled. The production experience should describe itself as one evolving MONOLITH voice and never as a complete browser emulation.
